# Software and epidemiology review — 2026-11-14

Instructor reference; contains scenario spoilers.

## Scope and method

The review covered every file in `R/`, `scenarios/`, `starter/`, `tests/`,
`scripts/`, and `www/`, plus the CI workflow, Dockerfile, and docs. Each
suspected defect was confirmed against both authored seeds before any code
changed, and the app was then driven in a headless browser through a full
intermediate run.

Overall: the design is sound. Truth, recalled, and collected data stay separate.
Time, missingness, and frozen snapshots behave the way a real investigation
does. Session restore validates its input carefully. The defects below were
specific and fixable. The second pass covers the larger design choices.

## Epidemiology: fixed

| # | Finding | Evidence | Change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E1 | An RSVP-only case definition classified ill walk-ins as **non-cases** and kept them in the denominators. A person criterion defines the cohort; it should not turn ill people into well ones. | Intermediate seed: 9 ill walk-ins of 25 counted as well. | Out-of-cohort guests are now `Excluded` and enter neither denominator. Preview, workbook notes, starter script, and restore checks know the new status. |
| E2 | The calculation check failed learners who used the right method with attack rates rounded to two decimals. The inputs step by 0.01, which invites exactly that. Feedback did not say which value was wrong. | Introductory seed: 4 of 5 foods failed with correctly rounded inputs. | The check accepts an RR within 0.05 of the exact value, or of the ratio of the learner's own correct rounded rates, and then shows the unrounded value. Feedback is per value. It names two common slips: a percentage, and the group's share of all cases used as an attack rate. |
| E3 | The leftover result said the isolate matched "the stool isolates" even when no stool samples existed. | Hold leftovers, test, never request stool. | A match is reported only when stool isolates exist. The stool result reports the match if the food result came first. The message adds that other items tested negative and that a negative food sample does not clear a food. |
| E4 | The confounding exercise showed stratum tables but no adjusted estimate, so learners could not compare crude with adjusted. | — | Added a Mantel-Haenszel pooled RR with a Greenland-Robins interval beside the crude RR. | E5 | No descriptive step. The curve used 12-hour bins for a 24-hour median incubation, dropped onsets outside the case window, and read live data while the rates table read the frozen snapshot. | — | New "Describe the cases" panel: symptom frequencies, incubation median and range, age, sex. | E6 | The true vehicle was listed first in every analysis tool, and the starter R script was pre-set to `chicken_salad`. The menu deliberately lists it last. | Selector, rates table, workbook, and strata picker all led with the vehicle. | Foods follow `menu_order` everywhere. | E7 | The process failure did not fit the agent. Slow cooling of cooked meat alone points to *Clostridium perfringens*. *Salmonella* needs a survival or contamination step as well; slow cooling then lets it multiply. The clinical picture (fever in 70%, 24-hour median incubation) is *Salmonella*. | — | Lou now cooks "in one big batch without checking its temperature" before the slow cooling. ## Second pass: every open recommendation implemented

The first pass left 17 design and architecture items for a decision. All are now
done. Each row names what changed and any limit that remains.

### Epidemiology

| # | Recommendation | What changed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Background symptoms in well guests | `background_illness` gives about 8% of spared guests unrelated mild illness, some before the meal. Introductory seed: diarrhea only 0.91 sensitive / 0.97 specific; standard 0.95 / 0.97; any symptom 1.00 / 0.92. |
| 2 | Non-response and under-reporting | 75 to 80% of cases phone in; 80 to 85% of well guests and 95% of ill guests answer the team. Results state how many answered. Response is fixed per guest. |
| 3 | Differential recall | `recall.rumour` lets ill guests over-report one food. The advanced scenario uses it: garden salad has a true RR of 1.5 and a recalled RR of 4.5. |
| 4 | Less tidy laboratory results | Stool culture is 70 to 85% sensitive, unrelated illness tests negative, and the leftover test can miss (80% in the advanced scenario). Messages say what a negative does and does not mean. |
| 5 | Onset as a date and time | Guests recall "Sunday at about 17:40". CSV and Excel carry `onset_time` and `report_time`; the starter script shows how to derive incubation. The case window stays in hours after the meal. |
| 6 | A contrasting agent | `potluck-03`: *Clostridium perfringens*, median incubation 12 hours, almost no fever, beef stew cooled slowly and only warmed through. |
| 7 | Evidence-based action conditions | Conditions read the record: two or more illness reports, a known preparation failure, or leftovers known to have gone home (new action). Feedback names the unmet condition. Continuing exposure at the hall stays false by design. |
| 8 | Menu leak | Without the menu, a guest interview records only the foods the guest names. |
| 9 | Small cells | Tables add Fisher's exact p-value and a plain note for small or empty cells. |

### Software

| # | Recommendation | What changed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Scenario prose in YAML | The briefing, cook's dish and account, locked dialogue terms, late-report rule, and food vocabulary come from the scenario file. A test fails if a food or clue word appears in `R/`. **Limit:** the cast (Pat, Lou) and the potluck frame stay in the app, because the welcome page and guidance text name them. |
| 2 | Split the server | `app_server.R` is now a 60-line assembly over seven `server_*.R` files, and the UI is one function per step. `style.css` is expanded. `lintr` runs in CI at 120 characters. **Limit:** these are plain functions sharing one context, not namespaced Shiny modules. Namespacing would rename every input and break saved sessions. Formatting is by hand and lint-checked; `styler` is not part of the toolchain. |
| 3 | Named guide steps | Code calls `step_index("weigh")`; the step count and checkpoint count are derived. Saved drafts keep their numeric keys so older files restore. |
| 4 | One guide validator | `validate_guide()` replaces the two copies. |
| 5 | Optional chat packages | `ellmer` and `shinychat` moved to `Suggests`; the app and tests run without them. **Limit:** both stay in `renv.lock` and the manifest. Removing them needs `renv::snapshot()` and a manifest rebuild under R 4.6.0. |
| 6 | Text limits | Checkpoints, actions, definitions, recommendations, and retries refuse text over 20,000 characters, the limit restore applies. |
| 7 | CI cache | The restored library is cached per R version and lockfile. |
| 8 | Readability | No text is below 12px; tables use plain headings and one cell per idea. |

Also fixed: CI's manifest check was failing on every branch because
`ubuntu-latest` had moved to a newer Ubuntu release, which changes the package
repository recorded in the manifest. The workflow now pins `ubuntu-24.04`.

### Compatibility

- For each seed, attendees, exposures, illness, onset of cases, and recalled
  foods are identical to the first release. The new options draw afterwards.
- Session files from before this change still restore. Their collected data
  remain valid; new state fields take their starting value.
- Two action labels changed: "Advise guests on hygiene and severe illness"
  (leftovers now have their own action) and "Recommend safe cooking and cooling
  guidance".
