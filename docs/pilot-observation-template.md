## Retry and transfer

- Decision retried:
- What changed and why:
- Did new evidence prompt revision of an unsupported conclusion?
- Could the learner name the remaining uncertainty?
- Retry duration (target five minutes or less):

Transfer prompt: At a different one-off gathering, the first callers all ate the
same dessert, but you have not spoken to anyone who stayed well. What would you
collect next, and what action can you justify now? Explain what the existing
reports do and do not establish.
